# BrightPath Youth Development Foundation – Research Notes

## Organisation status
BrightPath Youth Development Foundation is a fictional organisation created specifically for an academic website project. It is not presented as an existing registered non-profit organisation.

## Problem area
The concept focuses on youth employability, digital skills and career readiness in South Africa. The topic was selected because youth unemployment and youth disengagement from employment, education and training remain significant social and economic issues.

## Key evidence
- Statistics South Africa reported that the official unemployment rate for South Africans aged 15–34 was 45.8% in Q1 2026.
- Stats SA reported that 5.6 million young people aged 15–34 were employed, 4.7 million were unemployed and 10.6 million were outside the labour force in Q1 2026.
- In Q1 2026, the NEET rate for young people aged 15–34 reached 45.6% according to Stats SA's youth labour-market analysis.
- Stats SA's Social Profile of the Youth shows that 43.2% of youth aged 15–34 were not in employment, education or training in 2024.
- South Africa's National Digital and Future Skills Strategy implementation programme identifies digital and future skills as an important component of preparing young people for changing labour-market requirements.

## How the evidence informs the website
The website focuses on three fictional programme areas:
1. Digital Foundations – responds to the need for practical digital capability.
2. Career Ready – responds to employability and job-search preparation.
3. Mentorship & Growth – responds to guidance, confidence and goal-setting needs.
