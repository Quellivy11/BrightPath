Our purpose

Practical support for young people in Limpopo

BrightPath is a fictional community non-profit organisation created for this website project. The organisation focuses on practical pathways into further study, employment and entrepreneurship.

Our programmes combine digital literacy, career preparation and personal development so participants can build skills they can use beyond the classroom.
