About BrightPath

Helping young people build practical pathways forward.

We are a fictional youth-development non-profit created for this academic project.

Our story

From local need to practical action

BrightPath was established in 2022 as a fictional community initiative responding to the barriers young people can face when moving from school or tertiary education into the world of work.

The organisation began with small digital-skills workshops and developed a broader model that includes career preparation, mentorship and community learning.

Our approach is deliberately practical: participants should leave a programme with something useful, such as a stronger CV, improved digital capability, a clearer career plan or greater confidence in approaching opportunities.

Who we support

  • School leavers and recent graduates
  • Students seeking career preparation
  • Unemployed young adults
  • Young people building digital confidence
  • Volunteers and mentors

Mission

Equip

Equip young people with practical skills, guidance and confidence that can improve access to education, employment and entrepreneurship.

Vision

Opportunity

A community where young people can access the knowledge, resources and support required to build sustainable futures.

Values

Progress

We value inclusion, practical learning, accountability, respect, continuous improvement and community participation.
